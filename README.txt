# Afsana Mimi Portfolio — HTML and CSS

## About Me

I am Afsana Mimi, a data analytics and technical professional with an M.S. in Data Analysis and Visualization from the CUNY Graduate Center and a B.S. in Applied Mathematics from CUNY.

My portfolio showcases my academic projects, research, data analysis, interactive visualizations, research posters, and presentations. My work combines quantitative methods, database technologies, and visualization techniques to explore data, identify patterns, and communicate findings.

My research interests include Earth and environmental data science, oceanographic data analysis, geospatial analysis, population studies, and data visualization. I enjoy transforming complex datasets into meaningful insights through analytical methods and visual storytelling.
